# HER2CLIMB-02 Update

**ESMO Breast 2026**

Tucatinib + T-DM1 vs T-DM1 + placebo in HER2+ MBC

## Results
- PFS: 9.5 vs 7.4 mo (HR 0.74)
- CNS PFS: 7.8 vs 5.7 mo (HR 0.74)
- OS: 43.3 vs 41.0 mo (HR 0.98) ❌

## Takeaway
PFS and CNS benefit, but no OS benefit in current era with heavy T-DXd use after.
