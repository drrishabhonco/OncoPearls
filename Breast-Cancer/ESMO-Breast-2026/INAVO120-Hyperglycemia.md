# INAVO120 Subgroup Analysis - Hyperglycemia

**ESMO Breast 2026**

## Key Findings
**With Hyperglycemia**:
- mPFS: 21.0 vs 7.3 mo (HR 0.38)
- ORR: 64.7%

**Without Hyperglycemia**:
- mPFS: 12.8 vs 7.3 mo (HR 0.51)
- ORR: 59.3%

## Message
Hyperglycemia did **not** diminish benefit from inavolisib.
Manage glucose aggressively and continue treatment.
