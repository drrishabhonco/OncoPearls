# HER3-DXd (Patritumab Deruxtecan) in Metastatic Breast Cancer

**ESMO Breast 2026 | Phase II 422RO Study**

## Key Results

**ADC-Naïve Patients (Part A)**
- ORR: **35%** (HR+ ~39%, TNBC ~25%)
- Median PFS: **7 months**
- PFS-6: **50%**

**Prior Topo-1 ADC Exposed**
- ORR: **~5%**
- Median PFS: **1–3 months**

## Safety
- ILD: 4%
- 2 ILD-related deaths

## Clinical Pearl
HER3-DXd shows meaningful activity **before** prior exatecan-based ADCs. Strong cross-resistance after T-DXd/Dato-DXd/SG.
