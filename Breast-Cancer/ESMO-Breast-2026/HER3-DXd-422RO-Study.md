# HER3-DXd (Patritumab Deruxtecan) in Metastatic Breast Cancer
**ESMO Breast 2026 | Phase II 422RO Study**

## Key Takeaway
**HER3-DXd works best before prior topoisomerase-1 ADCs.**  
Strong cross-resistance seen after T-DXd / Dato-DXd / SG.

## Study Design
- 3 cohorts:
  - **Part A**: ADC-naïve (HR+/HER2- or TNBC)
  - **Part B**: Prior topo-1 ADC
  - **Part Z**: HER2+ after T-DXd

## Results

**ADC-Naïve (Part A)**
- ORR: **35%** (HR+ ~39%, TNBC ~25%)
- Median PFS: **7 months**
- PFS-6: **50%**

**Prior ADC Exposed**
- ORR: **~5%**
- Median PFS: **1–3 months**

## Safety
- ILD: 4%
- 2 ILD-related deaths

## Clinical Pearl
ADC sequencing matters more than we thought.  
“Not all ADCs are interchangeable.”
