# SATEEN Trial - SG + Trastuzumab after T-DXd

**ESMO Breast 2026**

## Key Results
- ORR: **3.7%**
- CBR (≥18 weeks): **14.8%**
- Trial stopped early for futility

## Takeaway
Limited activity of sequential TOP1-ADC strategies in heavily pretreated HER2+ MBC.

"ADC after ADC" is not automatically effective.
